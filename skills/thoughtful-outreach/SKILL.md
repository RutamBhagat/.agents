# Thoughtful Outreach

## Do

- "Your goal should be to have conversations with people at those salary levels."
- "I would ask them questions about these things and they loved it."
- "If you ask somebody a good question about a thing that they care about, one of two things happens."
- "In your video about X, I noticed you did Y."
- "I'm a bit confused because I thought Y was for Z."
- "Do you have any resources for me to better understand Z?"
- "Start with the context."
- "Give me a simple here's the thing that led me here."
- "Show where your misunderstanding is in the simplest teeniest part possible."
- "Then make the ask."
- "Point them in the right direction."
- "Come to me with the smallest possible ask."
- "Simple, clear, concise communications that are relevant to the person you're reaching out to are so important."
- "She was always so concise and clear with everything she did."
- "She was more likely to drop a useful link."
- "She was so useful and asked the right questions and brought the right resources."
- "Hey, I get specific error when trying to do specific thing."
- "I tried common debug step to no avail."
- "I saw two other users with this problem."
- "How can I help fix it?"
- "Realize there's a human on the other side perceiving the thing."
- "Thanks so much. This helped a bunch of things click for me."
- "I wrote a blog post detailing the parts I struggled with."
- "Would you be down to give it a quick look?"
- "Be present in the space."
- "Be part of the conversation."
- "Ask interesting questions."
- "Do your best to take advantage of the resources that people like me create."
- "Very calmly, kindly, and thoughtfully, reach out to the right people."
- "The restraint you use for that outreach is as important as the quality of the outreach itself."
- "The way you hit me up is as important as me being the right person to hit up."
- "Come to me with a really clear small ask."
- "Hitting me up with a solution to a problem I have."
- "Hey, big fan. I noticed you were fighting with TypeScript errors in your editor."
- "I built this little plugin that makes them look like this."
- "Link to the GitHub repo."
- "He solved a real problem in a super practical way and communicated it incredibly well."
- "Put so much effort into every word of his DM to me."
- "Communicate well."
- "Put effort into solving problems."
- "Identify the right problems."
- "Show the work for those solutions in a way that's considerate."
- "Be thoughtful in how you learn and grow."

## Don't

- "I'm tired of all the crazy like essays in DMs I get."
- "Hey, Theo, I was hoping I could ask you about something."
- "Here's my life story."
- "This is the format of the majority of the DMs I get."
- "None of this is about their journey."
- "None of this is a story about their life."
- "If you're only reaching out to me because you think I can get you a job, I don't want to talk to you."
- "Treat these senior engineers like boxes that you throw data at and hope you get some data back."
- "You're trying to use the people who are higher up than you."
- "Don't waste my time when you do it."
- "You DM me a life essay about helping you get a job."
- "Don't expect GitHub to be a magical thing to get you a job."
- "Don't hunt for GitHub repos to spam with crappy PR so that you can claim you contributed to things."
- "Certainly not saying spam random famous people in the space with your dumb questions."
- "Don't find someone to spam with every single question as you're going."
- "I'm certainly not saying spam us."
