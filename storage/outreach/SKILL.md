# Reaching Out to People

## Do

- "Your goal should be to have conversations with people at those salary levels to be part of the conversation with them."
- "I just knew they owned the thing that I was interested in or excited about and I would ask them questions about these things and they loved it."
- "If you ask somebody a good question about a thing that they care about, one of two things happens."
- "In your video about X, I noticed you did Y. I'm a bit confused because I thought Y was for Z. Do you have any resources for me to better understand Z?"
- "This is phenomenal. I will remember you if this is how you DM me."
- "Start with the context."
- "Give me a simple here's the thing that led me here."
- "Show where your misunderstanding is in the simplest teeniest part possible and then make the ask."
- "It is starting with the context that led them to my DMs."
- "clarifying what they're here for and what the the missing piece is"
- "then explicitly asking not for me to personally teach it to them, but to point them in the right direction."
- "The reason they're here is clear."
- "The reason they're in my DM specifically is clear."
- "The thing they need is clear, and the ask is super clear and low effort and low friction for me."
- "This is an awesome DM."
- "you come to me with the smallest possible ask, which is a link to a thing where you can learn more about it."
- "simple, clear, concise communications that are relevant to the person you're reaching out to are so important."
- "She was more likely to drop a useful link than she was to drop an essay about some shit."
- "She was so useful and asked the right questions and brought the right resources"
- "hey, I get specific error when trying to do specific thing. I tried common debug step to no avail. I saw two other users with this problem. How can I help fix it?"
- "it's crazy how easy it is once you realize there's a human on the other side perceiving the thing."
- "Thanks so much. This helped a bunch of things click for me. I know it's a bit of a big ask, but I wrote a blog post detailing the parts I struggled with. Would you be down to give it a quick look?"
- "be present in the space."
- "be part of the conversation, ask interesting questions, and don't waste my time when you do it."
- "Do your best to take advantage of the resources that people like me create."
- "when you've reach the limits of those resources, very calmly, kindly, and thoughtfully, reach out to the right people to do it."
- "the restraint you use for that outreach is as important as the quality of the outreach itself."
- "The way you hit me up is as important as me being the right person to hit up."
- "you've already looked through all your resources and can't find anything and you come to me with a really clear small ask."
- "Awesome. Do that all day."
- "hitting me up with a solution to a problem I have."
- "Hey, big fan. I noticed you were fighting with TypeScript errors in your editor. I do all the time. I built this little plugin that makes them look like this screenshot of what they look like. Link to the GitHub repo."
- "It was like three sentences, not even."
- "he solved a real problem in a super practical way and communicated it incredibly well."
- "he puts so much effort into every word of his DM to me."
- "It's clear he can communicate well and show value while also providing solutions to real problems."
- "if you can communicate well, if you can put effort into solving problems, identify the right problems, and show the work for those solutions in a way that's considerate, you're going to kill it."

## Don't

- "I'm tired of all the crazy like essays in DMs I get."
- "Hey, Theo, I was hoping I could ask you about something. Here's my life story. I started coding when I was four paragraphs later."
- "This is the format of the majority of the DMs I get. What the fuck?"
- "Notice how none of this is about the person."
- "None of this is about their journey."
- "None of this is a story about their fucking life."
- "If you're only reaching out to me because you think I can get you a job, I don't want to talk to you."
- "if you treat these senior engineers like boxes that you throw data at and hope you get some data back, you're not being a good junior."
- "You're trying to use the people who are higher up than you."
- "This message is someone trying to use me to make money."
- "spam GitHub with crappy open source repos and spam maintainers with crappy poll requests."
- "don't waste my time when you do it."
- "you DM me a life essay about helping you get a job"
- "don't expect GitHub to be a magical thing to get you a job."
- "don't hunt for GitHub repos to spam with crappy PR so that you can claim you contributed to things."
- "Certainly not saying spam random famous people in the space with your dumb questions."
- "I want to make sure people like myself are treated as a last effort resource"
- "don't reach don't find someone to spam with every single question as you're going."
- "If you're hitting me up because I'm famous and rich, fuck you."
- "I'm certainly not saying spam us."
